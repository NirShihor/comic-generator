Spanish reading practice — through comics

Don’t try to translate every word. Start with the pictures. Look at what’s happening, read the Spanish and see how much of the story you can understand.

Choose from short Spanish stories for beginner and intermediate learners. Read a real page from a Comigo comic, with translation and vocabulary help when you need it.

Why use comics for Spanish reading practice?

Pictures give written language a situation — characters, expressions, actions and surroundings that can help you make sense of unfamiliar Spanish.
