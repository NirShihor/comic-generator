Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026 · Comigo for iOS

Comigo is made by an independent developer. The app is designed to work without user accounts, without advertising, and without third-party tracking. This page explains the small amount of data that is processed to make the app's features work, and nothing here will try to surprise you.

What Comigo does not do

  • No user accounts — we don't know who you are.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers — ever.

Your voice, during speaking practice

When you use a speaking exercise, the app records your answer and sends that audio clip over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI's transcription service to turn your speech into text so the app can assess your Spanish. The audio is processed and discarded: we do not store your recordings on our server, and OpenAI does not use data submitted through its API to train its models. The temporary recording on your device is deleted after processing.

Microphone access is optional — if you decline it or switch speaking exercises off, everything else in Comigo (reading, listening, silent practice) works normally.

Words and sentences you ask about

When you tap a word or sentence for an explanation, the text of that sentence is sent to our server and on to OpenAI to generate the explanation. This text comes from the comics themselves, not from anything you've written.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, practice scores, vocabulary, notebook entries and downloaded comics are stored only on your device (and in your own device backups). They are never uploaded to us.

Server logs

Like practically every internet service, our servers keep short-lived technical logs of requests (IP address, endpoint, timestamp) for reliability and abuse prevention. They are not used to profile you and are routinely discarded.

Service providers

  • OpenAI — speech transcription and language explanations, as described above.
  • Fly.io — hosts our server.
  • Tigris — delivers comic downloads (content files only).

Each receives only what is needed to perform its function.

Children

Comigo is intended for adults and older teenagers. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
