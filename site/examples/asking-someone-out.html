Transcript and translation
  1. Ah... ¿podemos hacerlo el viernes mejor?Ah... can we do Friday instead?
  2. No puedo el viernes.I can't on Friday.
  3. Vamos a casa de mi tía.We are going to my aunt.
  4. No importa...Never mind...
  5. No, no... ¡El sábado está bien!No, no... Saturday is fine!
  6. Iré a tu casa a las siete.I'll come to your house at seven.
  7. Nos vemos entonces.See you then.

Language notes
  • Ah... ¿podemos hacerlo el viernes mejor?Ah is an interjection expressing realization or transition. Podemos is the present tense of poder, indicating ability or possibility, and hacerlo translates to 'do it,' with a direct pronoun meaning 'it.'
  • No puedo el viernes.No puedo uses the present tense of poder in the first person to express inability. El viernes specifies the day, serving as the time reference for the refusal.
  • Vamos a casa de mi tía.Vamos is the present tense of ir, meaning 'we are going.' The phrase 'a casa de' indicates direction and shows possession with 'mi tía' meaning 'my aunt.'
  • No importa...No importa is an idiomatic expression meaning 'it doesn't matter.' The phrase implies a sense of dismissing an issue rather than a literal translation.
  • No, no... ¡El sábado está bien!The repetition of 'no' emphasizes the negation. ¡Está bien! indicates agreement, with está being the present tense of estar.
  • Iré a tu casa a las siete.Iré is the simple future tense of ir, meaning 'I will go.' A las siete specifies a time, indicating when the action will take place.
  • Nos vemos entonces.Nos vemos means 'we'll see each other.' This is a common way to say goodbye or to indicate a future meeting.
