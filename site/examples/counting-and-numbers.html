Transcript and translation
  1. Creo que este fin de semana...I think that this weekend...
  2. ... deberíamos visitar a la familia Saltitos.... we should visit the Saltitos.
  3. ¿Cómo llegaremos allí, papi?How will we get there daddy?
  4. Los cinco empezaremos aquí...The five of us will start here...
  5. Caminamos 852 metros de huida...We walk 852 flee meters...
  6. ... hasta que lleguemos a una altura de 2564 metros.... until we get to a height of 2564 flee meters.
  7. Este perro se está rascando mucho...This dog is really scratching...

Language notes
  • Creo que este fin de semana...Creo is the present tense of creer (to think), indicating a current belief or opinion.
  • ... deberíamos visitar a la familia Saltitos.Deberíamos is the conditional tense of deber (should), introducing a suggestion about a future action.
  • ¿Cómo llegaremos allí, papi?Llegaremos is the simple future tense of llegar (to arrive), showing an action that will happen.
  • Los cinco empezaremos aquí...Empezaremos is the simple future tense of empezar (to start), indicating the group's intention to start at a specific time.
  • Caminamos 852 metros de huida...Caminamos is the present tense of caminar (to walk), expressing a habitual or ongoing action.
  • ... hasta que lleguemos a una altura de 2564 metros.Lleguemos is the subjunctive present form of llegar, used here in a context that reflects a condition (until we reach).
  • Este perro se está rascando mucho...Se está rascando is the reflexive verb rascarse (to scratch oneself) in the present progressive, showing an ongoing action.
