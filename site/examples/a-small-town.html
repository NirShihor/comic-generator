Transcript and translation
  1. Territorio de Nuevo México, 1883New Mexico Territory, 1883
  2. Por fin... ya no queda mucho...At last... not far now...
  3. Puedo verlo...I can see it...
  4. Un pueblo pequeño...A small town...

Words on this page
  • Territorio — territory
  • Nuevo — new
  • México — mexico
  • 1883 — 1883
  • fin — end
  • ya — already
  • queda (quedar) — remains
  • mucho — much/a lot
  • Puedo (poder) — I can
  • verlo (ver) — to see it
  • pueblo — town
  • pequeño — small

Language notes
  • Territorio de Nuevo México, 1883'Territorio de Nuevo México' is a proper noun and includes a time reference with '1883'. This structure indicates a specific place and time.
  • Por fin... ya no queda mucho...The phrase 'Por fin' means 'At last,' indicating that something has been awaited. 'Ya no queda mucho' uses 'queda' in the present tense to show that it's still ongoing.
  • Puedo verlo...'Puedo' is the present tense of 'poder,' meaning 'I can.' The infinitive 'verlo' means 'to see it,' indicating what the speaker can do.
  • Un pueblo pequeño...The article 'Un' means 'a' and is used for singular, masculine nouns. The adjective 'pequeño' follows the noun 'pueblo' to describe it.
