En la habitación In the room

At night a frightened girl tells her mum where she saw ghosts. Practise short questions and place phrases: en el armario, debajo del escritorio, debajo de mi cama, detrás del sofá.

Transcript and translation
  1. Mamá, mamá...Mum, mum...
  2. ¿Qué pasa?What's wrong?
  3. Vi un fantasma...I saw a ghost...
  4. ¿Fantasmas? No hay fantasmas.Ghosts? There are no ghosts.
  5. Sí, los hay...Yes, there are...
  6. En el armario...In the closet...
  7. Debajo del escritorio...Under the desk...
  8. Debajo de mi cama...Under my bed...
  9. ¡Y detrás del sofá!And behind the sofa!

Words on this page
  • Mamá — mum
  • mamá (mamáfade) — not applicable
  • Qué — what
  • pasa (pasar) — happens
  • Vi (ver) — I saw
  • fantasma — ghost
  • hay (haber) — there are
  • fantasmas (fantasmafade) — not applicable
  • Sí — yes
  • armario — closet
  • Debajo — under
  • escritorio — desk
  • cama — bed
  • detrás — behind
  • sofá — sofa

Language notes
  • Mamá, mamá...The repetition of 'mamá' adds emphasis and urgency, making it sound more affectionate or desperate.
  • ¿Qué pasa?¿Qué pasa? uses the present tense to ask about a current situation or event.
  • Vi un fantasma...Vi is the preterite form of ver, indicating a completed action that happened in the past.
  • ¿Fantasmas? No hay fantasmas.No hay is a negative construction meaning 'there are not'; it uses the present tense to indicate a current state.
  • Sí, los hay...Sí, los hay uses 'los' as a pronoun to refer to known plural items, and 'hay' means 'there are'.
  • En el armario...En el armario means 'In the closet', with 'en' indicating location.
  • Debajo del escritorio...Debajo del escritorio uses 'debajo' meaning 'under', which describes the position of something.
  • Debajo de mi cama...Debajo de mi cama has 'de' indicating possession, meaning 'of my bed'.
  • ¡Y detrás del sofá!¡Y detrás del sofá! uses 'detrás' for 'behind', and the exclamation adds excitement or urgency.
