Staying safe

An intermediate Spanish reading exercise: a real page from Una alianza improbable, from the El superviviente collection — an original Comigo comic, voiced line by line.

Look at the whole page first — who is there and what is happening? Then read the Spanish. Tap a bubble to hear it, and tap any word for its meaning.

Click on a bubble

Every line is voiced — tap a bubble to listen, tap a word for its meaning.

👆 Tap a speech bubble

Y tú los mantendrás a salvo. — And you will keep them safe. Y ayudarán a mantenernos a salvo... — And they will help keep us safe... Tiene razón. Por eso nos quedamos... — He's right. That's why we stayed... ¿Y cómo sugieres que hagamos eso? — And how do you suggest we do that? Deja que yo me preocupe por los detalles. — Let me worry about the details.
