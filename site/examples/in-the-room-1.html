En la habitaciónIn the room

A beginner Spanish reading exercise: a real page from Leer el ambiente — an original Comigo comic, voiced line by line.

Look at the whole page first — who is there and what is happening? Then read the Spanish. Tap a bubble to hear it, and tap any word for its meaning.

Click on a bubble

Every line is voiced — tap a bubble to listen, tap a word for its meaning.

👆 Tap a speech bubble

Mamá, mamá... — Mum, mum... ¿Qué pasa? — What's wrong? Vi un fantasma... — I saw a ghost... ¿Fantasmas? No hay fantasmas. — Ghosts? There are no ghosts. Sí, los hay... — Yes, there are... En el armario... — In the closet... Debajo del escritorio... — Under the desk... Debajo de mi cama... — Under my bed... ¡Y detrás del sofá! — And behind the sofa!

This is only one page of the story.

In Comigo, the story continues. Read the next page, listen to the characters, reveal translations when you need them and practise the Spanish you’ve just encountered — and discover more features.
