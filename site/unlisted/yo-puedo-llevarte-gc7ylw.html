Transcript and translation
  1. Yo necesito llegar al trabajo…I need to get to work...
  2. Yo puedo llevarte.I can drive you.
  3. Yo debo llegar a tiempo.I must get there on time.
  4. Lo sé.I know.
  5. Yo pienso que deberíamos irnos.I think we should get going.

Words on this page
  • necesito (necesitar) — need
  • llegar — to get
  • trabajo — work
  • puedo (poder) — can
  • llevarte (llevar) — to drive you
  • debo (deber) — must
  • tiempo — time
  • sé (saber) — I know
  • pienso (pensar) — think
  • irnos (irse) — get going

Language notes
  • Yo necesito llegar al trabajo…Necesito is the present tense of necesitar, indicating a current need or necessity.
  • Yo puedo llevarte.Puedo is the present tense of poder, showing ability in the present moment.
  • Yo debo llegar a tiempo.Debo is the present tense of deber, expressing obligation in the present.
  • Lo sé.Lo is a pronoun that refers to a specific thing, and sé is the first person present form of saber, meaning to know.
  • Yo pienso que deberíamos irnos.Pienso is the present tense of pensar, used to express an opinion, and deberíamos is the conditional form of deber, indicating a suggestion.
