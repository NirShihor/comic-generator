No estás estudiando. Estás leyendo. You're not studying. You're reading.

Original Spanish comics you read, hear and speak. Every bubble is voiced, every word explains itself when you tap it — and you keep going because you want to know what happens next.

How one story teaches Spanish

🔊

Read & listen

Follow the story bubble by bubble, every line spoken with tone and character. English is there when you want the safety net — and gone when you don’t.

👆

Never get stuck

Tap any word and it explains itself — meaning, grammar, the exact form in that sentence — then keeps reading with you.

🎙️

Say it back

Speak the dialogue and Comigo listens and tells you how you did. Hands-free with headphones, so practice fits a walk, a commute, a queue.

One story. Five ways to learn.

Finish an episode and it becomes your practice — every exercise is built from the sentences you just read. Off-screen modes are fully hands-free: headphones in, phone in your pocket.

Another chapter. Not another lesson.

Most language apps ask you to complete one more lesson. Comigo gives you one more chapter. You don’t learn Spanish so that one day you can enjoy stories — you enjoy stories, and the Spanish follows. No streaks, no drills for their own sake. Made for adults and older teenagers who dropped every flashcard app they ever tried.
