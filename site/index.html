No estás estudiando. Estás leyendo. You're not studying. You're reading.

Comigo is a library of original Spanish comics — westerns, mysteries, time travel, an alien at the breakfast table. Every bubble is read aloud, every word explains itself when you tap it, and every episode ends with its own listening and speaking practice. It reads like fun. It builds real Spanish.

How a page works

🔊 Panel 1 · Read

Read & listen

Follow the story bubble by bubble with native Spanish audio. Show or hide the English translation whenever you want — or never look at it at all.

👆 Panel 2 · Tap

Tap to understand

Any word, tapped, explains itself — meaning, grammar, the form used in that exact sentence — and saves to your notebook for later.

🎙️ Panel 3 · Speak

Say the line

Speak the dialogue and Comigo listens and grades you. Hands-free with AirPods, so practice fits a walk, a commute, a queue.

Finish the episode. Keep the Spanish.

Every episode ends with its own practice suite, built from the story you just read. The off-screen modes are fully hands-free — AirPods in, phone in your pocket.

Not another language app.

Comigo is a comic-reading experience that happens to leave Spanish behind. No streaks, no owl, no sentence about the horse drinking milk. Just stories you actually want to finish — made for adults and older teenagers who dropped every flashcard app they ever tried.

Native-voice audio, per bubble

Every line performed with tone and character — not text-to-speech monotone.

Reveal or hide translations

English is there when you want the safety net and gone when you don't.

Listen & Speak practice

Hear the line, say the line, get graded — Spanish→Spanish or English→Spanish.

A notebook that fills itself

Words you tap and lists you find in the story are saved for self-testing.

Interactive story pages

Buttons, screens and clues hidden in the artwork open into the language.

Beginner and intermediate levels

Series are levelled, so the next episode is always within reach.
